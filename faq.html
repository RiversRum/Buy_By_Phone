Frequently asked questions

How Buy By Phone works — buying your home a share at a time, with no mortgage, no interest and no debt.

The basics

What is Buy By Phone?›

Buy By Phone is a new way to buy your home gradually. You buy a share of your home to start with and move in, then buy more of it whenever you choose — right from your phone. You own the share you've bought outright, with no mortgage, no interest and no debt, and you pay rent only on the share you don't own yet.

Is this a mortgage or a loan?›

No. There's no mortgage, no interest and no debt. You buy your share outright. The only regular payment is rent on the part of the home you don't own yet.

Do I actually own my home?›

Yes — you own the share you've bought, and it's recorded legally through your solicitor. As you buy more, your share grows, and you can go all the way to owning 100%.

Is this a government scheme, like Shared Ownership?›

No. Buy By Phone is its own product from Lenuity, not a government scheme. Buying a home in shares isn't a new idea — what's different is that you can buy more of your home quickly through an app, without the legal process and valuation costs that usually come each time you want to increase your share.

How is it different from renting?›

The difference is ownership. With Buy By Phone you own a share of your home from day one, you can add to it whenever you like, and the more you own, the less rent you pay.

Getting your first home

How do I buy a home with Buy By Phone?›

You choose a home from a participating development and buy your first share through a solicitor, just like a normal home purchase. Your solicitor checks your identity and sets up your account. Once you complete, you move in — and from then on you can buy more of your home through the app.

How much do I need to start?›

You start by buying an initial share — a small portion of the home's value — plus the normal costs of buying a home. Because there's no mortgage, there's no separate mortgage deposit to save on top.

What kind of homes can I buy?›

To begin with, new-build homes from the housebuilders we work with. We'll add more homes and locations over time.

Who is it for?›

It's designed for first-time buyers who can comfortably afford the rent and want to start owning without a big deposit and mortgage. You'll go through the usual affordability and identity checks.

Rent, and buying more of your home

Why do I pay rent?›

You pay rent only on the share of the home you don't own yet — it's for living in that part. Rent and buying equity are two separate things: rent is for occupying, and buying more equity is how you increase what you own.

Will my rent change?›

Rent is reviewed from time to time and can change. The key thing in your control: the more of your home you own, the less rent you pay — so buying more is how you bring your rent down.

How do I buy more of my home?›

Through the app, whenever you choose, for as much or as little as you like. Small amounts are quick — just a few taps. Larger purchases involve a proper valuation and a few checks, so they're a bit more considered — but you don't go through the whole legal process again each time.

Can I own 100% of my home?›

Yes. You can keep buying more at your own pace, all the way to owning your home outright. There's no obligation to buy more, and no set schedule — you choose if and when.

Costs and Stamp Duty

What does it cost?›

You pay for your initial share plus the normal costs of buying a home, such as your solicitor's fees. There's no mortgage, so no interest. Any reservation fee you pay to secure the home is put toward your purchase, and some housebuilders may cover certain costs as an incentive.

Are there fees to buy more of my home?›

Buying more is designed to be simple and low-cost. Small purchases are quick and easy; a larger purchase may need a professional valuation.

Is there Stamp Duty?›

Buying a home can involve Stamp Duty, just like any purchase. How and when you pay it is something you decide with your solicitor, based on your own circumstances and the rules at the time — it's a tax matter for you and your solicitor, not something Buy By Phone sets. Your solicitor will guide you.

Ownership, security and moving on

Is my ownership protected?›

Yes. Your share is recorded legally through your solicitor, so what you own is clear and protected — just like any property purchase.

Can I sell or move?›

Yes. You can sell your home, or buy the remaining share and own it outright, and move on like any homeowner.

What if my circumstances change and I can't pay?›

There's no obligation to buy more — the share you've bought is yours, owned outright and debt-free, so buying more is always your choice.

And if you ever struggle to pay your rent, we'd work with you to find a way through — the same responsible approach any landlord should take.
